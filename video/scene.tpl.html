02 ER diagram

16 tables, 19 relationships

Read in three steps.

Specialization

PERSON is the superclass of DONOR, RECIPIENT, STAFF. Overlapping and partial: one table per subclass, sharing the key.

Multivalued attributes

A donor has many phones and attends many camps. Each gets its own table, keyed on both columns.

From donation to patient

DONATION → BLOOD_UNIT → ALLOCATION → REQUEST. UNIQUE(unit_id): a unit is reserved at most once.

03 Normalization

Write down the dependencies

donor_id → name, sex, dob, blood_group camp_id → name, date donation_id → donor_id, camp_id, date unit_id → donation_id, component, expiry component → shelf_days donor_id ↠ phone donor_id ↠ camp_id

Why 4NF

One table for phones and camps forces a row for every pair.

Add one phone number, and every camp needs a new row. Forget one, and the table claims a false fact.

One deliberate redundancy

A unit's blood group is copied from the donor once, onto its label.

It is the printed bag label and never changes, so it cannot go inconsistent, and the hot query skips a join.
